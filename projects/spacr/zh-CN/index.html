spaCR

CRISPR-Cas9 筛选的空间表型分析。

spaCR 在显微图像中分割细胞、细胞核和病原体，测量每个对象，并将测量结果与从同一孔中测序得到的向导 RNA 关联起来。它从板图像和 FASTQ 读段出发，最终得到单细胞测量值、训练好的分类器和基因排序列表。

  • 版本 1.5.1.4
  • 发布于 2026-10-09
  • Python 3.9–3.14

spaCR 的功能

七个模块，按顺序运行。每个模块读取上一个模块的输出，并将自己的输出写入同一个项目文件夹。分割、测量、标注和分类在没有测序数据的情况下也能运行。

  1. Import将显微镜文件转换为每块板一个的文件夹结构。产出： 图像文件夹和一个项目
  2. Mask用 Cellpose 模型分割细胞、细胞核、寄生虫和细胞器。产出： 每个视野的标签掩膜
  3. Measure测量每个对象的形状、强度、纹理和位置。产出： measurements.db 和单个对象的裁剪图
  4. Annotate在以键盘操作的网格中标注对象裁剪图。产出： 项目数据库中的标签
  5. Classify训练图像模型（ResNet、MaxViT）或测量值模型（XGBoost、随机森林）。产出： 附留出集指标的检查点，每个对象的得分
  6. Map barcodes从 FASTQ 读段中解码行、列和向导条形码。产出： 每个孔的向导 RNA 计数
  7. Regression根据得分和向导 RNA 计数估计向导效应和基因效应。产出： 火山图和命中排序列表
  8. 更多功能剂量反应、控制图、噬斑实验和募集实验、图像 UMAP、批次校正、统计功效与板布局设计，以及质控面板。参见实验与质控和表型。

输入

  • TIFF、OME-TIFF、ND2、CZI 和 LIF 图像，支持二维、三维体数据和时间序列
  • 任何能写出上述格式之一的显微镜所拍摄的多孔板
  • 带有行、列和向导条形码的 FASTQ 读段
  • 在 ND2、CZI、LIF 和 OME-TIFF 文件写入时实时监视文件夹（alpha 版，默认关闭）
  • 通过可选扩展支持 OME-Zarr 和 OMERO

生物

  • 宿主细胞中的 Toxoplasma gondii：向导、募集、噬斑和复制实验
  • 任何细胞类型均可用于分割、测量和分类
  • 其他病原体的页面正在开发中，默认隐藏

输出

  • 每个项目一个 SQLite 数据库：测量值、标签、预测结果
  • 以文件形式保存在磁盘上的掩膜、裁剪图和图像
  • 效应估计值、p 值和 q 值、诊断信息和运行记录
  • PNG、PDF、SVG 或 TIFF 格式的图；每张图一个 zip 文件，包含其数据、统计结果和重绘该图的脚本
  • AnnData 导出，以及 vvvv 导出（alpha 版）

模型

16 个附模型卡的已发布 Cellpose-SAM 和 YOLO 模型：寄生虫空泡、噬斑、噬斑实验孔，以及仅凭单一染色即可预测细胞、细胞核或寄生虫的跨通道模型。权重从 Hugging Face 下载，并与已发布的校验和进行核对。参见模型。

运行方式

  • 桌面应用程序适用于 Windows、macOS 和 Linux 的安装程序，或在用 pip 或 conda 安装后运行 spacr。
  • Python API每个模块都是一个接受设置字典的函数。
  • 命令行spacr-run 可在工作站、服务器或集群上以无界面方式运行任何流程。
  • 笔记本代码仓库中提供每个步骤（从掩膜到回归）的 Jupyter 笔记本。
  • DockerGitHub Container Registry 上的 CPU 和 CUDA 镜像。参见Docker。

流程

用一个完整视野贯穿 spaCR 全流程：感染 Toxoplasma 的 HeLa 细胞，MTOC 筛选的第 2 板、P08 孔、第 16 视野，用 Hoechst 和 CellMask 成像。步骤 1 至 4 使用该视野；步骤 5 是 GFP-TSG101 示例筛选的命中列表，因为 MTOC 筛选的命中结果未公开。

  1. 1图像

    2000 × 2000 px 视野的这一窗口中有 13 个宿主细胞和 54 个空泡；Hoechst 为蓝色，CellMask 为灰色。

  2. 2Mask

    细胞、细胞核和空泡轮廓：该视野中共有 37 个细胞、44 个细胞核和 130 个空泡。

  3. 3Measure

    按空泡覆盖率（Measure）着色的细胞：13 个中有 9 个高于 0，最高 0.161。

  4. 4Classify

    仅基于宿主特征的 XGBoost 感染得分；13 个细胞中有 8 个高于 0.5，该视野未用于训练。

  5. 5命中

    四板 GFP-TSG101 筛选：414 个基因，其中 4 个在 q < 0.05 下判定为命中；GRA14 即为命中。

数据：来自 cross-channel-cell-from-hoechst、cross-channel-nuclei-from-cellmask 和 cross-channel-toxoplasma-from-cellmask（CC BY 4.0）的 Hoechst 和 CellMask 图像及已发布的细胞、细胞核和空泡掩膜。Measure 和 XGBoost 分类器在 spaCR 中于 CPU 上对同一板的 52 个视野运行。命中列表：spacr-example-screen 的 OLS 回归。

  • Mask用 Cellpose 模型分割细胞、细胞核、病原体和细胞器。
  • Measure每个对象的形状、强度、纹理和位置，以及每个对象的裁剪图，存储在 SQLite 中。
  • Annotate在网格中用键盘快捷键标注对象裁剪图。
  • Classify训练图像分类器或测量分类器，并在每个检查点中保存留出集上的性能。
  • Map barcodes将测序读段分配到孔和向导 RNA。
  • Regression估计向导、基因和条件的效应及其区间，并给出命中排序列表。

How to read these scores
  • Precision is the share of objects the model reported that match a reference object; low precision means false detections. Recall is the share of reference objects the model found; low recall means missed objects.
  • F1 is the harmonic mean of precision and recall, so it is high only when both are. Either alone can look near-perfect: a model that reports one obvious object has high precision, and one that reports every dark blob has high recall.
  • IoU (intersection over union) is the overlap between a predicted and a reference object divided by the area the two cover together. F1 at IoU 0.5 counts an object as found when that ratio reaches 0.5.
  • AJI (aggregated Jaccard index) adds up the overlap of every matched pair and divides it by their combined area plus the area of unmatched objects, so merged, split and missed objects all lower it.
  • Dice is twice the overlap divided by the predicted area plus the reference area.
  • The grey bar is the baseline named in the column header, scored on the same test images: stock Cellpose-SAM without fine-tuning, or an earlier model where no stock model has the class.
  • The held-out test set is images kept out of training and used only for scoring.

Plaque-well detection

The plaque module first finds each well in a plate image or published figure and then segments plaques inside it; the detected well also gives the diameter that makes plaque areas comparable across microscopes. Stock YOLO has no plaque-well class, so each detector is compared with the one before it: on 129 held-out test images v2 scores mAP50 0.946 against v1's 0.884, with 49 false boxes on no-well images against 152.

Held-out test set: 73 clean validation images; incumbent validation images after removing exact and canonical-figure duplicates; validation data, not an independent test

Baseline: previous version (well detector v1, the YOLO11n incumbent) on the same validation images. Base model: yolo11n

On 3 Gel Doc validation plates mAP50-95 rises from 0.895 to 0.931; both models recover the expected well count on all 3.

Previous versions: v1, v2

How to read these scores
  • mAP at IoU 0.5 is the mean average precision of the predicted boxes when a box counts as correct at an IoU (overlap divided by the area the two boxes cover together) of 0.5 or more. mAP at IoU 0.5 to 0.95 averages it over ten thresholds, so loosely placed boxes score lower.
  • Precision is the share of predicted boxes that match a reference box; low precision means false boxes. Recall is the share of reference boxes the model found; low recall means missed wells.
  • The grey bar is the baseline named in the column header, scored on the same test images: stock Cellpose-SAM without fine-tuning, or an earlier model where no stock model has the class.
  • The held-out test set is images kept out of training and used only for scoring.

Cross-channel models

Cross-channel models predict one object type from another channel's image, so a stain can be left out and its channel used for another marker: cells from Hoechst alone, nuclei from a cell mask alone, and Toxoplasma vacuoles from either. Stock cpsam_v2 does not do this: on held-out fields its F1 at IoU 0.5 is 0.301, 0.201, 0.022 and 0.002 against 0.870, 0.888, 0.606 and 0.569 for the four trained models.

Held-out test set: 463 held-out fields; well-grouped held-out split (no well on both sides), hosts HFF, HeLa and THP1, fields without parasites included; the split also selected the checkpoint

Baseline: stock cpsam_v2. Base model: cpsam_v2

How to read these scores
  • Precision is the share of objects the model reported that match a reference object; low precision means false detections. Recall is the share of reference objects the model found; low recall means missed objects.
  • F1 is the harmonic mean of precision and recall, so it is high only when both are. Either alone can look near-perfect: a model that reports one obvious object has high precision, and one that reports every dark blob has high recall.
  • IoU (intersection over union) is the overlap between a predicted and a reference object divided by the area the two cover together. F1 at IoU 0.5 counts an object as found when that ratio reaches 0.5.
  • F1 at IoU 0.75 counts an object only when the ratio reaches 0.75, so the outline has to fit more closely.
  • Average precision at one IoU threshold is true positives divided by true positives, false positives and false negatives together. mAP averages it over several thresholds, so it also rewards outlines that fit closely.
  • AJI (aggregated Jaccard index) adds up the overlap of every matched pair and divides it by their combined area plus the area of unmatched objects, so merged, split and missed objects all lower it.
  • Dice is twice the overlap divided by the predicted area plus the reference area.
  • The grey bar is the baseline named in the column header, scored on the same test images: stock Cellpose-SAM without fine-tuning, or an earlier model where no stock model has the class.
  • The held-out test set is images kept out of training and used only for scoring.

筛选结果

在 Toxoplasma gondii 中进行的混合 CRISPR 筛选，寻找影响宿主 ESCRT 蛋白 GFP-TSG101 向寄生虫空泡募集的寄生虫基因，使用 spaCR 分析。出自 spaCR 预印本。

  • 4筛选板
  • 3经置换检验超过 FDR 5% 和效应阈值的基因
  • p 8.4 × 10⁻⁵GRA14 相对 SAG1 的募集

置换检验

spaCR 对本筛选 MaxViT 单细胞得分进行的向导 RNA 置换分析（Regression，非参数推断）：在控制板、行和列的条件下，计算每个基因和每条向导 RNA 与孔平均得分的偏相关，并与 2,000,000 次板内 Freedman–Lane 置换（随机种子 0）比较。版式与预印本图 S7（R 和 S 子图）相同。按示例自带的设置，对照孔和对照向导 RNA 保留在分析中，因此数值与预印本不同；预印本排除了它们并对孔进行了过滤。数据：spacr-example-screen，设置来自 spacr-example-hit。

表型

每个测量过的细胞都可以连同其图像裁剪一起放到二维图上，从而找到相似细胞群并用肉眼检查。

  • 5,000已映射细胞
  • 6聚类

分类

区分本筛选对照孔的分类器，均用 spaCR 在 CPU 上训练和解释：一个基于单细胞测量值的 XGBoost 模型，以及一个基于单细胞裁剪图的小型图像模型（MobileNetV3-small，8 个训练轮次）。第 1 列的孔携带靶向 SAG1 的向导 RNA（阴性对照），第 2 列的孔携带靶向 GRA14 的向导 RNA（阳性对照）。留出以整孔为单位，因此测试细胞与训练细胞不会来自同一个孔。数据：spacr-example-screen 和 spacr-example-hit，第 1 板，CC BY 4.0。
