Microscopy projects

Software, models and hardware for microscopy

The spaCR and ParaScape pages are public. The Timepose and Cellect pages are drafts and require a password.

Projects

  • spaCR

    A Python package and desktop application for image-based pooled CRISPR screens: segmentation, single-cell measurements, annotation, classification, barcode mapping and regression.

    Version 1.5.1.6

  • ParaScape

    A desktop app for exploring gene evidence in Toxoplasma gondii and Plasmodium falciparum: expression, fitness screens, localization, protein features, interactions and literature annotations.

    Version 0.0.6.0

  • Timepose

    Segmentation and tracking of cells in time-lapse microscopy.

    Password

  • Cellect

    A 3D-printed mount that holds a phone over a microscope eyepiece.

    Password

Biography
Einar B. Olafsson is a Research Investigator in the Department of Microbiology & Immunology at the University of Michigan. He studies how Toxoplasma gondii interacts with the cells it infects, combining CRISPR screening, high-content microscopy and AI-based single-cell image analysis to link genetic perturbations to spatial cellular phenotypes. He created and maintains spaCR. He studied biology and immunology at Uppsala University and received his PhD in Molecular Biosciences from Stockholm University, with a thesis on signalling in Toxoplasma dissemination by infected immune cells.
